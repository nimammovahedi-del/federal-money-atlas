      About

      What this measures

      A prototype, not yet published. Numbers come from public data for fiscal year 2023.

      Money that came back

      Federal contracts, grants, and Social Security & SSI that agencies committed in fiscal year 2023 for work or payments in each county, from USAspending.gov.

      Taxes residents paid

      Income tax comes from IRS county data. Payroll, corporate, estate and excise taxes are estimated from wages, investment income and population, then scaled to the U.S. Treasury's totals.

      Typical county vs. U.S. overall

      The typical county ($1.09) is the one in the middle. The U.S. overall figure ($0.74) adds up every county; it is lower because a few high-income places pay much of the tax. County pages compare with the typical county.

      What's estimated

      Medicaid and other statewide grants have no county, so each county gets a share based on population. These are marked est.

      Why the U.S. figure is under $1

      Medicare, veterans' payments, federal salaries, defense overhead and interest on the debt aren't tied to a county here.

      Three map views

      Land shows counties at real size. People sizes each county by its residents. Grid combines nearby counties into equal squares. Connecticut reports 9 planning regions and isn't drawn yet.
