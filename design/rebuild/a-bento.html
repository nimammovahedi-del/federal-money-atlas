About

What this site measures

Federal money that reached each county in fiscal year 2023 — Social Security, grants and contracts — compared with the federal taxes its residents paid.

Status

Prototype

Not published. Numbers from Phase 0.

Counted

Money in

Contracts, grants and Social Security & SSI committed in FY2023 for work or payments in the county (USAspending.gov).

Counted

Taxes paid

Income tax from IRS county data, plus payroll, corporate, estate and excise taxes estimated from wages, investment income and population, scaled to Treasury totals.

Two benchmarks

U.S. overall vs. typical county

The U.S. overall figure ($0.74) adds up every county. The typical county ($1.09) is the one in the middle. County pages compare with the typical county.

Estimated

Statewide grants

Medicaid and other statewide grants have no county, so each county gets a population share. They're marked "est."

Not counted

Why the U.S. figure is under $1

Medicare, veterans' payments, federal salaries, defense overhead and interest on the debt aren't tied to a county here.

Map

Land, People, Grid

Land shows real county sizes. People sizes each county by its residents. Grid combines nearby counties into equal squares. Connecticut reports 9 planning regions and isn't drawn yet.
