Design notes

This prototype combines four of the seven directions: Atlas (the look and the map), Ledger (money in vs. taxes paid), Statement (the county detail) and Explorer (its own page). Here is what survived and what was cut.

The pages

Map (home)From the Atlas: the Land / People map, search, and three ways in.
County pageLedger summary on top, Statement detail below, the Atlas "next door" box at the side. Printable.
ExploreFrom the Explorer: measures, side panel, rank, pin-to-compare, shareable link. Not the home page.
Method(not drawn) Every rule, estimate and source. Linked from every number.

Kept, merged and cut

FromPartDecisionWhy
AtlasLand / People mapKeep (home + Explore)The most important honesty fix: most people live in small, dense counties.
AtlasPaper, charcoal ink, Barlow / Barlow Condensed, sage-to-forest map scaleKeep as the whole site's lookCalm and neutral; one type family keeps four ideas looking like one site.
Atlas"Next door" neighborsMerge into county page sidebarThe comparison people instinctively make, without ranking the whole country.
AtlasPlate numbers, print-atlas decorationCutCharm without information.
LedgerMoney in vs. taxes paid, side by side, on one bar scaleKeep as the county page's top summaryAnswers the headline question in one glance, before any table.
LedgerGreen = money in, umber = taxes, everywhereKeepTwo fixed meanings across bars and table headings; neither is a party color.
LedgerTwo-sided "above/below the middle" mapCutClashes with the Atlas map and invites a winners-and-losers reading.
LedgerFlow (Sankey) diagram; dollar "balance" lineCutA lot of work for little extra; a dollar balance reads as "owed".
StatementPeriod covered + "data as of" header; itemized sections; this year / 5-yr average / changeKeep as the county page bodyThe structure that trends and corrections need.
StatementAdjustments: cancellations and money taken backKeep (Phase 1 data)The feature no competitor has at county level.
StatementPrint layoutKeepCouncil meetings, newsrooms, classrooms.
Statement"Statement" title, mono fonts, state index homeCutSounds like a bill or an official document; Explore replaces the state index.
ExplorerMeasure chips, panel, rank, compare up to 3, shareable URLKeep as its own pagePower users get depth without crowding casual visitors.
ExplorerExplorer as the home page; its own palette and fontCutThe home page should answer one question, not offer a cockpit.
AllEstimated numbersMarked with an "est." tag and striped barsThe reader always sees what is measured and what is estimated.

Not in this prototype yet

5-year history, cancellations, top recipients, the method page, share images, Connecticut on the map (the base map still has its old counties), and search by ZIP code.
