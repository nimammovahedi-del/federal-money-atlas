About this prototype

A working sketch of v1, built on Phase 0 data. The design reasoning is in the case study.

What's counted

Money in: federal contracts, grants and Social Security & SSI committed in fiscal year 2023 (Oct 2022 – Sep 2023) for work or payments in the county, from USAspending.gov. Grants to statewide programs such as Medicaid have no county, so each county gets a population share of them, marked est.

Taxes paid: income tax from IRS county data, plus payroll, corporate, estate and excise taxes estimated from wages, investment income and population. All are scaled to Treasury's totals; 97% of federal receipts are assigned to counties.

Not counted here: Medicare, veterans' and other direct payments, loans (they're repaid), federal salaries, defense overhead and interest on the debt. That's why the U.S. average is below $1.

Three ways to see the map

Land draws every county at its real size. People redraws each county as a circle sized by its population, so dense places get their fair share of the picture. Grid groups nearby counties into equal-size cells and colors each cell by its combined total, which smooths out tiny counties with extreme values.
